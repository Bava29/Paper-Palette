OUR STORY

More than stationery, a place to begin.

Paper & Palette was created for people who believe that the right tools can make everyday ideas feel a little more special.

From a simple notebook filled with thoughts to a blank sheet waiting for its first sketch, we believe creativity often starts with something small.

That's why we carefully bring together stationery, art supplies and creative essentials that are beautiful, practical and made to be enjoyed every day.
